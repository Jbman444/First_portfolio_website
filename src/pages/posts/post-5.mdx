## Overview
This project investigates how biofilm development alters flow and transport behavior in porous media systems. Using pore-scale simulations and image-based analysis, the work explores how biological growth modifies hydraulic pathways and impacts mass transport.

## Methods
- Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) simulations of pore-scale flow
- Image processing and segmentation of experimental data
- Velocity field and transport analysis
- Python-based data processing and workflow automation

## Tools & Skills
CFD (OpenFOAM), Python, Image Processing, Data Analysis, Scientific Visualization

## Key Outcomes
- Quantified impact of biofilm growth on permeability and flow distribution
- Developed reproducible workflows for simulation and analysis
- Integrated experimental imaging with numerical modeling
