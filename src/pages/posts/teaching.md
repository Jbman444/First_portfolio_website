## Overview
My outreach efforts focus on increasing access to engineering education and helping students—particularly from underrepresented backgrounds—navigate academic and professional pathways. Through mentorship, campus engagement, and technical instruction, I’ve worked to make both college and advanced engineering concepts more approachable and attainable.

## Methods
- Peer mentorship and academic guidance for high school and undergraduate students  
- Development of introductory technical materials (e.g., CFD fundamentals)  
- Hosting campus visits and interactive outreach events  
- Translating complex engineering topics into accessible, real-world concepts  
- Supporting student-led learning through project-based instruction  

## Tools & Skills
Public Speaking • Mentorship • Science Communication • Curriculum Development • OpenFOAM • Python • GitHub • Technical Instruction

## Key Initiatives

### Future Aggies Program – Student Outreach
- Returned to :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1} to mentor students through the Future Aggies program  
- Shared firsthand experience transitioning from high school to engineering studies  
- Provided guidance on financial aid, academic preparation, and student life in engineering  

### AVID Road Trip Program
- Hosted and guided three campus tours for students in the AVID program  
- Introduced underrepresented students to college environments and opportunities  
- Facilitated discussions on navigating higher education and building academic confidence  

### Graduate-Level Instruction – Computational Fluid Dynamics
- Designed and led an introductory program on computational fluid dynamics (CFD) concepts  
- Taught students how to develop term projects using OpenFOAM and Python  
- Created structured learning materials and example workflows hosted on GitHub  
- Focused on building intuition in fluid mechanics alongside practical simulation skills  
- Course materials: https://github.com/Jbman444/OpenFoamTraining.git  

### Graduate Ambassador – Providing resources to undergrads
- Connected undergraduate students with graduate school opportunities  
- Provided insight into research pathways, applications, and academic career development  
- Mentored students exploring advanced study in water resources engineering
